# PRODUCT.md — Reelbox

## Product Purpose

A native Android movie & TV browser powered by TMDB. Shows what's trending, in theaters, coming soon, and — crucially — where each title is legally streaming in your region with deep links to providers. No piracy, no unauthorized embeds, no accounts required.

## Users

- Movie/TV enthusiasts who want a clean, dark-mode catalog browser
- People frustrated by fragmented streaming availability across services
- Users who prefer native apps over web interfaces
- Privacy-conscious users (no login, no tracking, local-only watchlist)

## Brand Voice

- **Cinematic, confident, restrained.** Dark theater aesthetic. No noise.
- **Honest.** "This app does not host or stream any video" — stated plainly.
- **Utility-first.** Every element helps you decide what to watch and where.
- **No marketing fluff.** No "discover a world of entertainment" copy.

## Tone Principles

- Short sentences. Active verbs.
- Technical accuracy over persuasion.
- Dark mode is not a theme — it's the only mode. Cinema doesn't have lights on.
- Red accent (Accent #FF4B4B) for action, gold (#FFC94A) for ratings, violet (#8B5CF6) for secondary actions.

## Anti-References

- No gradient text, no glassmorphism, no hero-metric template
- No "Get started" CTAs that lead to signup walls
- No rounded-everything Material defaults — sharp, intentional corners
- No loading skeletons that flash — honest loading states

## Register

brand

## Strategic Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Screenshots > feature lists.
2. **Region-aware by default.** Ghana (GH) is the default — not US.
3. **Legal streaming is a first-class feature.** "Where to watch" section gets equal billing with cast/similar.
4. **Offline-first mindset.** Watchlist persists locally. No cloud sync needed.
