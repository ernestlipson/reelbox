How it works

Three steps, then you're watching

  1. Pick your region

    The pill in the top bar opens a list of twelve countries. Default is Ghana, and the choice is remembered next launch.

  2. Browse or search

    Scroll the home rows or type into search. Results are debounced, so the app stays responsive on a slow connection.

  3. Tap a provider

    Every logo opens the service in JustWatch and hands off to the installed app when one exists. Nothing is hosted or embedded.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Does Reelbox stream or host any video?

No. It shows metadata from TMDB and links out to services that legitimately hold the licence. Nothing is embedded, indexed or mirrored.

Where does the streaming availability come from?

TMDB's watch-providers endpoint, which is sourced from JustWatch. The app only reads it — it doesn't scrape or re-publish provider catalogues.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server-side profile. Your watchlist lives in SharedPreferences on the device and nothing is uploaded.

What happens when a title isn't available in my region?

The panel says so plainly instead of showing an empty row. Switch the region from the pill and the app re-fetches availability for that country.

Does it work offline?

The catalog needs a connection. The watchlist and your region choice are local, so they survive a cold start with no network and stay readable.

Why isn't Reelbox on the Play Store?

Reelbox isn't on the Play Store yet, so the release build is signed with the project's own key and distributed directly. It installs fine once you allow your browser to install unknown apps.

Installing takes three steps

  1. Allow installs. Open Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser or file manager.
  2. Download the APK and confirm the install prompt. It doesn't come from the Play Store, so Android will warn you once.
  3. Open Reelbox. No account, no onboarding wall — you land straight on the home rows. Pick your region from the pill in the top bar.
